Steal this / skip this

Steal this

  • Umber text on cream (≈10:1 contrast) — “old” never has to mean unreadable.
  • One ornament system: ❧ ✦ ¶ — use it for dividers only, never decoration soup.
  • Candlelight gradient: every sheet slightly brighter at top-center.
  • Small caps + generous letter-spacing for labels; the period reads as engraved.

Skip this

  • Blackletter everywhere — Fraktur in a nav bar is a costume, not a style.
  • Full-color photos: duotone them to umber or don’t ship them.
  • Gold gradients + drop shadows = wedding-invite kitsch. Gold lives in hairlines.
  • Heavy noise (opacity > 8%): it reads as dithering, not vellum.

Go looking

Are.na “parchment / vellum” · study folios at the British Library · brand references: Aesop apothecary pages, Penguin clothbacks, Chobani Heritage labels, whisky “cask” pages · keywords: manuscript marginalia, deckled edge, iron gall ink, letterpress.
